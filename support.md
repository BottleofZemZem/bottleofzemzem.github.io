# Real Tuner Pro — Support

A chromatic and by-string tuner for iPhone and iPad. If something is not
working, the answers below cover almost everything. Anything else, write to
asgaryuksel3@gmail.com and you will get a reply.

## The app shows nothing when I play

Check the signal level in the bottom right corner. If it reads a very low
number, the app is not hearing you: move closer to the microphone, or check
that nothing is covering it.

The detector needs a sustained pitch. Clapping, tapping and speech are rejected
on purpose, because they have no stable frequency to measure. Play a note and
let it ring.

## I denied microphone access and cannot get the prompt back

iOS only shows that prompt once. Open the app and use the button on the
permission screen, which takes you straight to the right page, or go to
Settings > Privacy & Security > Microphone and turn Real Tuner Pro on.

## The reading jumps between two notes

That usually means two strings are ringing at once. Mute the strings you are
not tuning. If you want the tuner to stay on one note regardless, open the
Strings tab and tap a peg to lock it to that string.

## It says my string is in tune when it is not

Make sure the reference pitch is where you expect. Settings > Reference Pitch
is 440 Hz by default and adjustable from 415 to 466 Hz in half-hertz steps; if
it was changed, every reading shifts with it.

Also check the tuning preset. Settings > Tuning has Standard, Drop D, Half Step
Down, Full Step Down, Open G, DADGAD, and four- and five-string bass.

## Can I tune something other than a guitar?

Yes. The Tuner tab is fully chromatic and names whatever note it hears, so it
works for violin, ukulele, mandolin, banjo, cello and anything else with a
pitch. The Strings tab is where the guitar and bass presets live.

## Tapping a peg plays a sound. Can I turn that off?

That is the reference tone, so you can hear the note you are tuning to. It
plays only when you tap a peg. While it sounds, pitch detection is muted, so
the app does not hear its own speaker and report the string as perfect.

## Does it work without internet?

Always. The app contains no networking code and never connects to anything. It
works in airplane mode and will keep working with no connection at all.

## What happens to the audio?

It is analysed on your device as it arrives and discarded. Nothing is recorded,
stored or sent anywhere. See the https://bottleofzemzem.github.io/
