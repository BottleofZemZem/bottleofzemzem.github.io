# Privacy Policy — Real Tuner Pro

**Last updated: 24 September 2026**

## Summary

Real Tuner Pro does not collect any data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no
advertising, and no networking code of any kind. Nothing you do in the app
leaves your device, because the app has no way to send it anywhere.

## The microphone

Real Tuner Pro asks for microphone access, because measuring the pitch of an
instrument is the only thing it does.

Audio from the microphone is analysed on your device, in memory, as it arrives.
Each short window of sound is turned into a frequency reading and then
discarded. Audio is never written to storage, never kept after the reading is
taken, and never transmitted. There is no recording feature, and no audio
leaves the app.

You can withdraw microphone access at any time in iOS Settings > Privacy &
Security > Microphone. The app will stop working until you grant it again,
which is expected: without a microphone there is nothing to measure.

## Settings stored on your device

Your preferences — reference pitch, tuning preset, accent colour, haptic
feedback, and which microphone input to use — are saved in the app's own local
storage on your device. They are not synced, backed up to us, or shared. They
are removed when you delete the app.

## No third parties

The app contains no analytics SDKs, no advertising SDKs, no crash reporting,
and no social or login integrations. No third party receives data from this
app, because the app sends data to no one.

The app makes no network requests. It works with the device in airplane mode.

## Data we hold about you

None. There is no server, no database and no account system associated with
this app. We cannot identify you, and we have nothing to look up, export or
delete on request.

If you download the app from the App Store, Apple handles that transaction and
may collect information under its own privacy policy. That relationship is
between you and Apple; we receive no personal data from it beyond the aggregate
sales figures Apple shows every developer.

## Children

The app is safe for all ages and collects nothing from anyone, children
included. It contains no advertising, no purchases and no links out of the app.

## Your rights

Privacy laws such as the GDPR and the CCPA give you rights to access, correct,
delete and port your personal data, and to object to its processing. Those
rights apply to data a company holds about you. We hold none, so there is
nothing for us to produce or erase. Everything the app stores is on your own
device and under your control; deleting the app removes it.

## Changes to this policy

If the app ever starts collecting data, this policy will be updated before that
version ships, and the change will be described in the release notes. The date
at the top of this page shows when it was last revised.
